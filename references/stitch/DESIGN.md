## Brand & Style

The design system embodies haute couture craftsmanship, tactile sophistication, and quiet luxury. Designed for high-profile clients, fashion houses, bridal patrons, and commercial creative directors, the visual language mirrors the tactile precision of high-end editorial artistry.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Style:** Contemporary Editorial Minimalism infused with classical atelier sensibilities.
- **Atmosphere:** Serene, intimate, luminous, and intentional. The interface serves as a bespoke gallery frame, stepping back to let hyper-detailed photography, skin textures, and artistic portfolio works command attention.
- **Rhythm:** Generous breathing room, unhurried visual cadence, museum-grade layout discipline, and razor-sharp typographic alignment.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural materials of an atelier—fine pigments, polished stone, and organic undertones.

- **Primary (`#1A1715`) — Deep Obsidian Charcoal:** Replaces stark black with an organic, ink-rich charcoal for primary typography, sharp hairline frames, and high-impact booking triggers.
- **Secondary (`#C9A88E`) — Soft Champagne Rose Gold:** A delicate metallic hue used for luminous highlights, subtle decorative numerals, focus states, and bespoke accents.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6A58`) — Warm Terracotta Umber:** An earthy, muted tone used for editorial tags, category subtitles, and interactive hover shifts.
- **Neutral (`#F8F5F0`) — Alabaster Sand:** A warm, luminous canvas background that prevents the clinical glare of pure white while enhancing high-fashion photography.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F8F5F0` (Alabaster base)
- **Surface Raised / Card:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure linen contrast)
- **Surface Muted / Layered:** `#F0ECE4` (Soft sand container)
- **Border / Hairline:** `rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.08)` (Delicate structure)

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts **Bodoni Moda**—a high-fashion editorial serif with razor-thin horizontal serifs and graceful vertical weight—with **Manrope**—an architectural, ultra-clean humanist grotesque.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Bodoni Moda regular weight. Use title case or sentence case for headlines. Never apply tight uppercase treatments to Bodoni Moda.
- **Labels, Dates & Badges:** Rendered in Manrope, uppercase, tracking expanded between `0.08em` and `0.16em` to create haute couture micro-typography.
- **Editorial Quotations & Captions:** Emphasize italicized Bodoni Moda for model descriptions, client quotes, and campaign titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an asymmetric editorial grid system reminiscent of fine art coffee table books and luxury print issues.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid columns constrained to a max-width of `1440px`. Column gutters set to `2.5rem`, outer margin `4rem`. Asymmetric column spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text block aligned alongside 7-column image spreads) give layouts rhythm.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid margins at `2.5rem` and gutters at `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Strict 4-column system with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding.

### Layout Principles
- **Air & Suspension:** Sections transition with `space-2xl` to create unhurried pauses between portfolio themes, client accolades, and booking inquiries.
- **Imagery Aspect Ratios:** Default to `4:5` vertical portraiture, `3:4` beauty close-ups, and sweeping cinematic `16:9` lookbook banners.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids artificial, generic drop shadows. Depth is achieved entirely through tonal layering, translucent glass scrims, and gossamer-fine outlines.

### Architectural Hierarchy
1. **Flat Base Ground:** The warm Alabaster canvas (`#F8F5F0`) sits beneath all editorial elements.
2. **Elevated Panels & Cards:** Solid linen white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed by micro-hairlines (`rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.08)`).
3. **Floating Overlay & Booking Drawer:** Ambient feathering using a diffused, warm-tinted shadow:
   - `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.03)`
4. **Interactive Scrims & Modals:** Backdrop filters featuring `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with an alabaster tint: `rgba(248, 245, 240, 0.85)`.
5. **Image Framing:** High-contrast imagery rests directly on surfaces without shadows, framed by clean negative space or microscopic single-pixel borders.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces sharp, crisp architectural precision (`roundedness: 0`).

### Shape Philosophy
- **Sharp Modernism:** All cards, portfolio image containers, buttons, inputs, and flyout navigation drawers carry crisp 90-degree right angles (`0px`).
- **Contrast & Softness:** Visual softness is introduced naturally through skin tones, fluid makeup swatches, organic imagery, and delicate serif curves—not rounded plastic UI corners.
- **Exceptions:** Miniature status indicators, color shade swatches, and circular thumbnail crops maintain true circular forms (`50%` radius).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Reserve / Book Session):** Solid deep charcoal (`#1A1715`) background, crisp rectangular silhouette, text in `#F8F5F0` using `label-md` uppercase. Padding: `1rem 2.25rem`. Hover introduces a subtle background transition to `#322E2B` with a gentle `0.5px` border shift.
- **Secondary Action (View Lookbook):** Transparent background with a `1px` solid border in `#1A1715`. Hover transitions background to `#1A1715` with text changing to `#F8F5F0`.
- **Text Link CTA:** Inline Bodoni Moda or Manrope with an animated hairline underline positioned `4px` beneath the text baseline, expanding on hover from 0% to 100% width.

### Portfolio Cards & Image Grids
- **Editorial Cards:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background or borderless flush image presentations. Captions sit outside the image container using `label-sm` category headers paired with `headline-sm` campaign titles.
- **Hover State:** Images apply a smooth, subtle zoom (`scale: 1.025`, duration: `600ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`) within an overflow-hidden frame.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Appearance:** Rectangular tags with `1px` border in `rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.12)`.
- **Active State:** Solid `#1A1715` with crisp white text.
- **Inactive State:** Background transparent, text in Tertiary `#8C6A58`. Padding: `0.5rem 1rem`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Treatment:** Minimalist bottom-line borders (`1px solid rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.2)`) on the canvas surface, or boxed inset inputs with `#FFFFFF` background.
- **Focus State:** Bottom-line sharpens to `#1A1715` with a subtle Champagne accent indicator (`#C9A88E`). Floating labels transition to `label-sm` tracking.

### Booking Drawer & Date Picker
- **Drawer:** Slides in from the right edge with a sharp-edged `#FFFFFF` container and soft diffused shadow.
- **Date Selector:** Crisp tabular calendar grid. Available session dates indicated by subtle dot markers in Champagne (`#C9A88E`); selected date forms an ink-black square tile with white text.

### Service & Pricing Accords
- **List Items:** Horizontal rows divided by `1px` borders in `rgba(26, 23, 21, 0.08)`. Expanding rows reveal session durations, shade preparation guidelines, and studio terms with `body-sm` typography and seamless accordion transitions.
